# Self-authored under explicit creative delegation

User evidence: a separate wedding decor site using the Ready Solutions category of catalog.ameli-rental.ru; product placeholders now, a future module from the rental engine later; products and cart must be visualized; other blocks delegated. Follow-up: use Scroll Craft, choose design systems from styles.refero.design, make it modern and expensive; use a subagent to develop a funnel based on audience, pain points and promises.

Authored eight-topic brief:
1. Mood: considered, intimate, contemporary, editorial. Refero Cosmos and Structured.
2. Journey: picture your wedding, choose a complete zone, understand composition, assemble quantities, discuss the date.
3. Energy: warm opening, quiet strip, involved catalog, intimate dark editorial, calm practical close.
4. Feeling curve: desire (real wedding photo); relief (preassembled sets); agency (usable catalog); confidence (composition and exceptions); clarity (process and FAQ); resolve (saved selection).
5. Signature: every selected set builds a photographic strip of the visitor's wedding and marks its corresponding zone. The same selection stays through checkout and export.
6. Aesthetic: editorial premium-minimal, without duplicating financial or SaaS patterns.
7. Distinct natural-flow scenes. No forced continuous flight or cinematic wait before shopping.
8. Assets: actual project photography and product renders from the user's rental catalog. No generation.

Grammar: gallery. It fits an image-led selectable collection. Filmic and continuous-world would block direct category access; chaptered editorial would postpone photos; live surface does not fit the emotional wedding brief; poster and cutlist put spectacle before selections; split stage would constrain catalog and mobile comparison.

Peak: “I picked a few things and could see my wedding coming together.” Catalog has the largest natural content span. No authored blank scroll.

Layer contract: far paper background, large photographic wedding scene, foreground detail photo that overlaps the main image, stable text outside imagery. Opening shows the complete title and CTA; midpoint shifts the foreground more than the far image; exit resolves into the usable catalog. Photos remain separate editorial frames, avoiding extracted-subject duplication.

Score: opening layered parallax; catalog natural flow with working selection; editorial media parallax and one-time text reveal; process staggered entry; FAQ native disclosures; close held static. Functional catalog takes precedence over the skill's four animation-family quota. No unused effects are added for a quota.

Fingerprint registry: new local workspace /tmp/ameli-scroll-work, no prior project rows. Existing unrelated projects were not treated as prior builds.

Preflight: Node and Chrome available. ffmpeg missing; no video encoding or video assets in this build, so the video-only dependency is unnecessary. No paid asset API used. Browser validation uses the provided CUA environment rather than a separate Playwright session.

Latest user steering: a multipage catalog, not a single landing. Preserve all six ready-solution category types from the rental site; future categories must be data-driven. Use Cyrillic Амели and Russian visitor-facing copy. Brand colors follow the original yellow, white and gray; no burgundy/cherry accent. This explicit brief overrides earlier independently authored palette choices.

Final palette refinement from user: do not repeat the exact bright yellow; use a related, more refined wedding shade in beige/gold. Applied muted gold and champagne over ivory.
